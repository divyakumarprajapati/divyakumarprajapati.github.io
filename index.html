Vulrend Founder 2026 → now

Making ads people actually want to see.

Vulrend takes a brand from product to published ad. It learns the brand, researches the market, plans the campaign, creates ads from the brand’s real product photos and video, writes the caption, hashtags and CTA, publishes on schedule and learns from what people respond to.

  • A seven-stage agentic pipeline, streamed live with a full audit trail
  • Five services on GCP with FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React and LangGraph, 800+ tests
  • One trusted ffmpeg engine renders every ad with voice-over and Whisper captions
  • Nine kinds of ads from the same photos, about $0.60 per ad

CourtNG Founder 2025 → now

Turning a camera into the best referee on court.

Great matches need great referees, and most courts never get one. CourtNG calls the lines, tracks the players and runs the whole match on-premise, fully offline.

  • YOLO and Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3), fine-tuned for tennis and pickleball
  • A match orchestration engine for calibration, ball tracking, rallies, scoring and rules
  • An LLM tool-calling agent that streams game events to an umpire client over WebSockets
  • Next: robots that learn a sport and become training partners for people
